THE FIELD GUIDE How to play & scoring

Aim at the birds and click or tap to fire. Your rifle follows your aim, with a short tracer and visible recoil on every shot. Each magazine holds six shots; press R or tap Reload for a fresh one. Empty magazines reload automatically.

Teal birds are worth 100, coral birds 150, and small golden birds 250. Build a hit streak for up to 5× points. Miss a shot or let a bird escape and your streak resets. Birds get faster and more frequent every 15 seconds.

Rounds last 90 seconds of active play. P / Esc pauses, M toggles sound. Switching tabs pauses automatically. Keyboard players: focus the game, use arrow keys to aim, and Space to fire. Stylized targets, no gore, no tracking, and no network required.
